Industries We Serve

Import planning built around your industry, not a generic shipment.

Industry-specific import planning that connects the product to its permits, customs treatment, handling risks and final Philippine execution.

Talk to The Mills

Sectors

Find the page built for what you move.

Construction & Infrastructure

We clear and deliver cranes, excavators, steel and project materials from permit to site, including oversized pieces, so crews and schedules never wait on equipment.

Renewable Energy & Power

We move solar, storage, wind and hydro equipment through permits, incentives and customs to the project site, so your build stays on its delivery window.

Data Centers & Digital Infrastructure

We clear and deliver servers, cooling, power and security equipment on schedule, handling batteries and mixed categories, so your facility goes live when planned.

Chemicals & Controlled Materials

We match your substance to the permits, controlled-chemical approvals and Dangerous Goods rules it needs, then clear and deliver it, so chemical shipments are accepted, not stranded.

Food, Beverage & Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)

Align Food and Drug Administration (FDA), labeling, tax, cold-chain and customs requirements so food, beverage and Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) arrive compliant and ready for the shelf.

Manufacturing & Industrial Machinery

We import and deliver production machinery with correct classification and valuation, packed and sequenced for installation, so your line starts on schedule.

Automotive, Heavy Equipment & Special-Purpose Vehicles

Know before you buy: we confirm a vehicle or equipment unit can be imported and what it will really cost once taxes and permits are in, then clear it and deliver it, so no unit is ever stranded at the port.

Pharmaceuticals, Medical Devices & Healthcare

We secure the right authorizations, clear medicines, vaccines and medical devices, and deliver them temperature-controlled, so patient-critical cargo is released fast and fully compliant.

Agriculture, Plants, Animals & Fisheries

We coordinate plant, animal and fishery clearances across every agency involved, so perishable and live cargo clears quickly and arrives in condition.

Telecommunications, Electronics & IT Hardware

We secure the approvals for radio equipment and handle batteries and classification, so connected devices and telecom hardware clear without holds and reach your channel on time.

Marine, Vessels & Yachts

We handle registration, customs and port requirements for yachts, vessels, landing craft tanks (LCTs) and marine equipment, so your asset is cleared, registered and operating without delay.

Aviation & Aerospace

We clear aircraft, engines and parts with the technical paperwork matched, and give Aircraft on Ground (AOG) shipments priority, so aircraft are back in service sooner.

Retail, Consumer Goods & E-Commerce

We clear apparel, appliances, beauty and consumer goods through the right product approvals and valuation, so inventory arrives compliant and ready to sell.

Cold Chain & Temperature-Sensitive Cargo

Reefer trucking, qualified cold storage and timely delivery, with permits and customs coordinated, so frozen, chilled and temperature-sensitive cargo stays in range from port to store.

Mining, Minerals & Industrial Materials

We move heavy equipment, controlled materials and mixed shipments through customs to remote sites, with explosives and reagent permits handled, so operations are not held up waiting for cargo.

Freight Forwarders & Logistics Providers

We clear your clients' cargo, including regulated shipments, with permits and authority handled, so you offer reliable clearance without building a customs team.
