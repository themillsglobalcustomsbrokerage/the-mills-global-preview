What Mills Customs Does

The Mills Customs manages import and export clearance as part of a wider compliance system. The service connects customs filing to importer accreditation, permits, tariff classification, valuation, origin, examinations, taxes, release, records and post-clearance exposure.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • Paperwork that does not matchInvoices, packing lists and permits that disagree with each other are a common reason cargo is queried.
  • Classification and valuation doubtsAn unconfirmed tariff code or declared value can change duties and trigger an examination.
  • Holds with no clear ownerWhen cargo is flagged, importers often cannot tell who is answering customs or what is still outstanding.

Core Support

Here's exactly what's included.

01

Import Clearance

We review the entry before it is lodged, then manage assessment, payment, examination and release, so your cargo leaves the port sooner.

02

Export Clearance

We prepare declarations, permits and origin documents together, so outbound cargo is released without last-minute holds.

03

Importer and Exporter Registration

We handle Bureau of Customs (BOC) and Client Profile Registration System (CPRS) registration, accreditation and updates, so you can file when you need to.

04

Tariff Classification

We confirm the correct ASEAN Harmonized Tariff Nomenclature (AHTN) or Harmonized System (HS) code and its permit treatment, so you pay the right duty and avoid reclassification disputes.

05

Customs Valuation

We review transaction data, additions, related-party terms and supporting documents, so the declared value holds up under assessment.

06

Duties and Taxes

We estimate duties and taxes before arrival and coordinate payment, so the landed cost holds and release is not delayed.

07

PEZA and Special Transactions

We prepare the documents for qualified imports, exports, transfers and tax-incentive transactions, so you keep the benefits you qualified for.

08

Examination and Hold Coordination

We respond to document requests, physical examination, x-ray and sampling, so a hold is resolved quickly and the cargo moves.

09

Post-Clearance Support

We organize records and run readiness reviews, so you are prepared if customs audits an entry later.

10

Customs Remedies

We coordinate the right professional response to manifest issues, abandonment, seizure and classification or valuation disputes, so a problem does not become a loss.

Common Questions

Answers before you have to ask.

As of 2026, the Bureau of Customs adopted a three-year validity framework for importer accreditation, subject to current rules, annual or continuing obligations and eligibility. Importers should verify their actual status and applicable renewal process before shipping.

The Mills can review product information and support tariff classification. Where certainty is commercially important, an application for an advance ruling from the Tariff Commission may be considered under the applicable process.

The Mills can organize the customs and documentary file and coordinate the appropriate response. Because seizure and forfeiture are legal proceedings, representation and legal remedies must be handled by duly qualified counsel under a separate engagement.

Release does not end the importer's obligations. The Bureau of Customs may review declarations and records after release, making accurate classification, valuation, permits and recordkeeping essential.
