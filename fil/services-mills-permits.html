Import Permits, Product Registration at Regulatory Approvals

Inaalam namin kung anong government approvals ang kailangan ng product mo at kami na ang nag-aasikaso ng applications, para hindi ma-hold ang goods mo dahil sa kulang na papeles.

Ano ang Ginagawa ng Mills Permits

Mina-map ng The Mills Permits ang bawat product at shipment sa mga applicable na regulatory requirement, at tinutukoy ang mga responsableng agency, kailangang approval, pre-shipment conditions, customs-aligned na documentation at mga tuloy-tuloy na obligasyon pagkatapos ng approval.

Dito madalas maipit ang clients

  • Hindi alam kung aling agency ang applicablePuwedeng sakop ng ilang regulator ang iisang product, at bihirang malaman sa commercial name kung sino.
  • Approvals na huli nang dumaratingMatagal makuha ang permits at registrations, at hindi puwedeng i-release ang regulated cargo kung wala ang mga ito.
  • Approvals na hindi tugma sa shipmentNagkaka-delay kapag iba ang permit details sa invoices, labels o customs entries.

Core Support

Ito mismo ang mga kasama.

01

Permit Mapping

Tinutukoy namin ang regulator, license, product registration, shipment clearance at supporting documents para sa product mo, para alam mo ang buong proseso bago ka bumili.

02

Coordination sa Food and Drug Administration (FDA)

Magbigay ng support sa License to Operate, Certificate of Product Registration at iba pang applicable na health-product authorization workstream.

03

Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Authority to Release Imported Goods (ATRIG) Coordination

Ihanda at i-coordinate ang mga application para sa Authority to Release Imported Goods para sa mga covered na item at excise-tax situations.

04

Agricultural Sanitary at Phytosanitary Requirements

I-coordinate ang mga requirement na may kinalaman sa Bureau of Animal Industry (BAI), Bureau of Plant Industry (BPI), Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), at National Meat Inspection Service (NMIS), kasama ang importer licensing o registration, sanitary at phytosanitary clearances, quarantine requirements at inspection, kung applicable. Para naman sa meat importation, ang NMIS ang naglilisensya sa mga qualified na meat importer, habang ang Sanitary and Phytosanitary (SPS) Import Clearance ay pinoproseso sa BAI.

05

Product Standards at Certification

I-coordinate ang Bureau of Philippine Standards ng Department of Trade and Industry (DTI-BPS) requirements para sa mga product na sakop ng mandatory certification, kasama ang Import Commodity Clearance (ICC), Philippine Standard certification, inspection, testing at supporting product documentation.

06

Telecommunications at Optical Media Requirements

I-coordinate ang mga registration, import permit at equipment approval na may kinalaman sa National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) at Optical Media Board (OMB), kasama ang NTC type approval, type acceptance o equipment conformity requirements at ang mga applicable na OMB import o export permit.

07

Environmental, Chemical at Controlled-Goods Requirements

I-coordinate ang applicable requirements na may kinalaman sa Environmental Management Bureau of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR-EMB), Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA), Dangerous Drugs Board (DDB), at Philippine Nuclear Research Institute of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST-PNRI) para sa regulated chemicals, controlled precursors, radioactive materials at environmentally regulated goods.

08

Specialized na Regulatory Requirements

I-coordinate ang mga permit, clearance at registration na may kinalaman sa Fertilizer and Pesticide Authority (FPA), Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA), Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA), National Tobacco Administration (NTA), Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA), Philippine Ports Authority (PPA), Fair Trade Enforcement Bureau of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI-FTEB), Land Transportation Office (LTO), Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP), Biodiversity Management Bureau of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR-BMB) at Forest Management Bureau of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR-FMB), depende sa product at shipment.

09

Renewals at Amendments

Tina-track namin ang permit validity, renewals at amendments, para hindi mag-lapse ang approvals at maharang ang susunod mong shipment.

Bakit Pinipili ng mga Negosyo ang The Mills

May coordinated plan ka, hindi hiwa-hiwalay na instructions mula sa mga ahensya.

  • Ang requirements ng mga agency ay pinagsasama sa iisang operational sequence.
  • Konektado ang permit review sa customs classification at shipment timing.
  • Nakapangalan sa bawat permit ang eksaktong regulator na responsable, walang malabong permit language.
  • Kahit approved na, nakikita pa rin ang continuing validity at renewal obligations.

Kino-coordinate ng The Mills ang permit assessment at applications pero hindi ito nag-i-issue o naggagarantiya ng anumang government approval. Nakadepende pa rin sa kasalukuyang batas at sa responsableng Philippine agency ang applicability, documentary requirements, inspections, processing at approval.

Mga Madalas Itanong

May sagot na bago mo pa kailangang magtanong.

Depende ang regulator sa identity ng product, composition, function, intended use, tariff classification at market activity. Nagsisimula ang The Mills sa technical product documents, hindi lang umaasa sa commercial name.

Hindi. Ang LTO ay karaniwang tungkol sa regulated establishment at sa authorized activity nito, habang ang Certificate of Product Registration (CPR) o iba pang market authorization ay para sa isang partikular na health product. Nakadepende sa FDA product category at current rules ang tamang pagkakasunod-sunod.

Hindi. Ang ATRIG ay para sa covered na imported articles at tax situations sa ilalim ng current BIR rules. Nire-review muna ng The Mills ang produkto at tax treatment bago mag-advise na kailangan ang ATRIG workstream.

Isa itong sanitary at phytosanitary clearance na ginagamit ng mga competent agricultural authority para sa covered na animal, plant, fishery at related products. Nakadepende sa commodity kung aling agency ang mag-i-issue at ano ang requirements.

May ilang approval na technically puwede pang i-process sa later stage, pero marami ang dapat meron na bago ang shipment, arrival o release. Inirerekomenda ng The Mills na i-confirm muna ang filing sequence bago i-dispatch ng supplier ang goods.

Walang iisang timeline para sa lahat. Depende ang tagal sa agency, application type, product risk, inspections, testing, quality ng documents at workload ng agency. Nagbibigay ang The Mills ng product-specific na dependency plan, hindi general na pangakong walang basehan.
