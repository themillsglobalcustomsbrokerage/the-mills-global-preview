Mga Madalas Itanong

May sagot na bago mo pa kailangang magtanong.

Ang customs broker ang naghahanda at nagfa-file ng import at export entries sa Bureau of Customs, nagka-classify ng goods, nagco-compute ng duties at taxes, kumukuha ng mga required na permit, at nagco-coordinate ng examination at release para makagalaw ang cargo.

Nag-o-offer ang The Mills Global ng customs brokerage at clearance, import permits, project cargo, marine services, dangerous goods handling, logistics, packaging, cargo insurance, time-critical freight at trade advisory.

Sineserbisyuhan ng The Mills ang data centers, renewable energy, construction, manufacturing, automotive, healthcare, food and beverage, agriculture, chemicals, electronics at telecom, marine, aviation, retail, cold chain, mining at freight forwarders.

Sabihin sa The Mills kung ano ang ii-import mo bago ito i-ship. Ire-review namin ang product, iko-confirm ang classification at permits, at ima-map ang plan, para pagdating ng shipment ay ready na ito for clearance.

May offices ang The Mills Global sa BGC, Taguig City at Binondo, Manila, at nakikipag-work sa mga partner sa mga major port at trade city sa buong mundo.
