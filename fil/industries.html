Mga Industry na Sineserbisyuhan Namin

Import planning na nakabase sa industry mo, hindi sa generic na shipment.

Import planning na specific sa industry, na nagkokonekta sa produkto sa mga permit, customs treatment, handling risks at final execution sa Pilipinas.

Kausapin ang The Mills

Sectors

Hanapin ang page na ginawa para sa cargo mo.

Construction at Infrastructure

Kini-clear at dine-deliver namin ang cranes, excavators, steel at project materials mula permit hanggang site, pati ang oversized na pieces, para hindi naghihintay sa equipment ang crews at schedules.

Renewable Energy at Power

Dinadala namin ang solar, storage, wind at hydro equipment sa permits, incentives at customs hanggang sa project site, para pasok pa rin sa delivery window ang build mo.

Mga Data Center

Kini-clear at dine-deliver namin on schedule ang servers, cooling, power at security equipment, kasama ang handling ng batteries at mixed categories, para mag-go live ang facility mo ayon sa plano.

Chemicals at Dangerous Goods

Tinutugma namin ang substance mo sa kailangan nitong permits, controlled-chemical approvals at Dangerous Goods rules, saka namin ito kini-clear at dine-deliver, para tanggapin ang chemical shipments at hindi maipit.

Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG)

I-align ang Food and Drug Administration (FDA), labeling, tax, cold-chain at customs requirements para dumating na compliant at ready i-display ang food, beverage at Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG).

Industrial Manufacturing

Ini-import at dine-deliver namin ang production machinery nang tama ang classification at valuation, naka-pack at naka-sequence para sa installation, para on schedule magsimula ang line mo.

Automotive

Alamin muna bago bumili: kino-confirm namin kung puwedeng i-import ang sasakyan o equipment unit at magkano talaga ang aabutin kasama ang taxes at permits, tapos ikini-clear at dine-deliver namin ito, para walang unit na ma-stuck sa port.

Healthcare at Pharmaceuticals

Kinukuha namin ang tamang authorizations, kini-clear ang medicines, vaccines at medical devices, at dine-deliver ang mga ito nang temperature-controlled, para mabilis at fully compliant ang release ng patient-critical cargo.

Agriculture at Fisheries

Kino-coordinate namin ang plant, animal at fishery clearances sa lahat ng ahensyang involved, para mabilis ma-clear ang perishable at live cargo at dumating itong nasa maayos na kondisyon.

Electronics at Semiconductors

Kinukuha namin ang approvals para sa radio equipment at kami ang bahala sa batteries at classification, para ang connected devices at telecom hardware ay ma-clear nang walang hold at makarating sa channel mo on time.

Marine at Offshore

Inaasikaso namin ang registration, customs at port requirements para sa yachts, vessels, landing craft tanks (LCTs) at marine equipment, para cleared, registered at operating ang asset mo nang walang delay.

Aviation at Aerospace

Kini-clear namin ang aircraft, engines at parts nang tugma ang technical paperwork, at priority sa amin ang Aircraft on Ground (AOG) shipments, para mas maagang makabalik sa serbisyo ang aircraft.

Retail at E-Commerce

Kini-clear namin ang apparel, appliances, beauty at consumer goods gamit ang tamang product approvals at valuation, para dumating ang inventory nang compliant at ready nang ibenta.

Cold Chain at Temperature-Sensitive Cargo

Reefer trucking, qualified cold storage at on-time delivery, na may coordinated permits at customs, para manatiling in range ang frozen, chilled at temperature-sensitive cargo mula port hanggang store.

Mining at Minerals

Dinadaan namin sa customs at dinadala sa remote sites ang heavy equipment, controlled materials at mixed shipments, kami na rin sa explosives at reagent permits, para hindi mabitin ang operations sa paghihintay ng cargo.

Freight Forwarders at Logistics Providers

Kini-clear namin ang cargo ng clients mo, pati regulated shipments, at kami ang bahala sa permits at authority, para makapag-offer ka ng maaasahang clearance nang hindi bumubuo ng customs team.
