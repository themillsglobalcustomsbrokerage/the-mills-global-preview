Common Challenges

Why Data Centers shipments usually get stuck.

Many Categories in One Project

Servers, batteries, generators and cooling each follow different rules. We separate and clear each category, so one regulated item does not hold the whole shipment.

Batteries and Dangerous Goods

UPS and backup batteries usually need Dangerous Goods handling. We arrange it before booking, so carriers accept these shipments.

Fit-Out Dates

Equipment that arrives late or damaged holds up commissioning. We protect and schedule each delivery to your fit-out dates.
