Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how The Mills Global Customs Brokerage Inc. collects, uses, discloses, and protects the personal information of website visitors when they access the company’s website or utilize its services. The Mills Global is committed to safeguarding privacy and ensuring the security of all personal data.

Information Collected

The Mills Global may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact Information. (i.e., name, email address, and company name)
  • Transactional Information. Details about services requested and transactions conducted through the company’s services
  • Technical Data. IP address, browser type, operating system, and other technical details related to website visits.
  • Usage Data. Information about how the website and services are used.

Use of Information

Collected personal information may be used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and manage customs brokerage services and related customer support
  • To communicate with website visitors, including responding to inquiries and delivering customer service
  • To analyze usage patterns and preferences in order to improve the website, services, and marketing efforts
  • To fulfill legal obligations and enforce the company’s terms and conditions

Disclosure of Information

Personal information may be shared with third parties under the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers and Partners: To those assisting The Mills Global in delivering services and operating its business
  • Legal Compliance: When required to respond to lawful requests, subpoenas, or court orders
  • With Consent: When website visitors provide explicit consent or direction

Data Security

The Mills Global implements appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. While robust measures are in place, it is important to note that no method of data transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

Website Visitor Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, website visitors may have rights to access, correct, or delete their personal information. To exercise these rights, they are encouraged to contact the company directly using the contact details provided below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in business practices or legal requirements. Website visitors are encouraged to review this policy regularly to stay informed of any updates.
