What Mills Permits Does

The Mills Permits maps each product and shipment against the applicable regulatory requirements, identifying the responsible agencies, required approvals, pre-shipment conditions, customs-aligned documentation and continuing obligations after approval.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • Not knowing which agency appliesThe same product can touch several regulators, and its commercial name rarely tells you which.
  • Approvals that arrive too latePermits and registrations take time, and regulated cargo cannot be released without them.
  • Approvals that do not match the shipmentPermit details that differ from invoices, labels or customs entries cause delays.

Core Support

Here's exactly what's included.

01

Permit Mapping

We identify the regulator, license, product registration, shipment clearance and supporting documents for your product, so you know the full path before you buy.

02

Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Coordination

Support License to Operate, Certificate of Product Registration and other applicable health-product authorization workstreams.

03

Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) Authority to Release Imported Goods (ATRIG) Coordination

Prepare and coordinate Authority to Release Imported Goods applications for covered articles and excise-tax situations.

04

Agricultural Sanitary and Phytosanitary Requirements

Coordinate requirements involving Bureau of Animal Industry (BAI), Bureau of Plant Industry (BPI), Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR), and National Meat Inspection Service (NMIS), including importer licensing or registration, sanitary and phytosanitary clearances, quarantine requirements and inspection, as applicable. For meat importation specifically, NMIS licenses qualified meat importers, while the Sanitary and Phytosanitary (SPS) Import Clearance is processed through BAI.

05

Product Standards and Certification

Coordinate Bureau of Philippine Standards of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI-BPS) requirements for products subject to mandatory certification, including the Import Commodity Clearance (ICC), Philippine Standard certification, inspection, testing and supporting product documentation.

06

Telecommunications and Optical Media Requirements

Coordinate registrations, import permits and equipment approvals involving National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) and Optical Media Board (OMB), including NTC type approval, type acceptance or equipment conformity requirements and applicable OMB import or export permits.

07

Environmental, Chemical and Controlled-Goods Requirements

Coordinate applicable requirements involving Environmental Management Bureau of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR-EMB), Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency (PDEA), Dangerous Drugs Board (DDB), and Philippine Nuclear Research Institute of the Department of Science and Technology (DOST-PNRI) for regulated chemicals, controlled precursors, radioactive materials and environmentally regulated goods.

08

Specialized Regulatory Requirements

Coordinate permits, clearances and registrations involving Fertilizer and Pesticide Authority (FPA), Philippine Coconut Authority (PCA), Sugar Regulatory Administration (SRA), National Tobacco Administration (NTA), Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA), Philippine Ports Authority (PPA), Fair Trade Enforcement Bureau of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI-FTEB), Land Transportation Office (LTO), Civil Aviation Authority of the Philippines (CAAP), Biodiversity Management Bureau of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR-BMB) and Forest Management Bureau of the Department of Environment and Natural Resources (DENR-FMB), depending on the product and shipment.

09

Renewals and Amendments

We track permit validity, renewals and amendments, so approvals do not lapse and block your next shipment.
