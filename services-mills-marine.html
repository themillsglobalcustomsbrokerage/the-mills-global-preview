What Mills Marine Does

The Mills Marine manages the importation and regulatory process for vessels and marine equipment, covering customs, permits, port requirements, registration and related operational needs. It is designed for commercial and leisure marine clients that need these requirements handled as one coordinated process.

Where clients usually get stuck

  • Vessel and equipment rules across agenciesCustoms, Maritime Industry Authority (MARINA), port and registration requirements all apply, and each has its own paperwork.
  • Spares needed fastA vessel waiting for parts cannot wait for requirements to be worked out from scratch.
  • Imported assets with no clear pathYachts, workboats and engines often arrive before the owner knows what must be registered or cleared.
